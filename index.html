    Hi, I’m Vilppu — a Mechanical Engineering student at Esslingen University of Applied Sciences with a strong interest in design, simulation, and building real-world prototypes.

    I enjoy bringing ideas to life: starting from a CAD model, testing and optimizing it, and ultimately turning it into something tangible. For me, engineering is all about connecting theory with hands-on problem solving.

    When I’m not working on technical projects, you’ll usually find me playing football, working out, or spending time outdoors — camping or fishing. I like the balance between structured engineering work and being active or in nature.

    Having grown up in different countries and speaking four languages (French, Finnish, German and English), I’m comfortable adapting to new environments and enjoy working in diverse, international teams.

    Always open to connect for future entry-level opportunities or exciting freelance projects.
